# MDRT (Million Dollar Round Table): membership standards, philosophy and development programs — input for a Thai GA agency-management app

> Research date: 24 Nov 2026. **Method caveat:** in this session every direct page fetch failed (mdrt.org, assets.mdrt.org, members.mdrt.org, thaipr.net, ctfassets.net are outside the session's allowed network domains). All findings below come from search-engine extracts of the cited pages, not from reading the full documents. Figures marked **[VERIFIED-snippet]** were quoted from an official MDRT document or press release in the search extract; figures marked **[UNVERIFIED]** come from third-party blogs or could not be tied to a membership year. **The official Thailand THB table was NOT readable** — see Gaps in Q1.

## Q1. MDRT membership requirements — 2026 membership year (2025 production) and 2027 membership year (2026 production); USD and THB; COT and TOT

### Takeaway
Official USD base figures are confirmed for both years (2026: USD 87,000 commission / USD 174,000 premium; 2027: USD 92,000 commission), but the official Thailand THB row could not be retrieved, so the app must treat THB thresholds as a configurable, admin-entered value per membership year rather than hard-coding them. COT = 3x and TOT = 6x MDRT is confirmed by Thai and third-party sources.

### Cited Findings
**2026 membership year (based on 2025 production)**
- [VERIFIED-snippet] "The 2026 commission requirement is USD 87,000"; premium method: "A minimum of USD 174,000 of eligible paid premium is required." — [MDRT, Membership Information for the 2026 Million Dollar Round Table](https://assets.mdrt.org/download/3af8200430fe11f0ba79fe135e3fbd63)
- [VERIFIED-snippet] MDRT publishes a table "Commission and Premium Production Requirements for Membership in the 2026 Million Dollar Round Table, based on 2025 production, expressed in local currency", with MDRT / COT / TOT columns for commission and premium, dated 12/15/2025. The Thailand row was not visible in the extract. — [MDRT local-currency requirements table 2026](https://assets.mdrt.org/download/4e6c5526d6c911f0aa5ce25980888793); [second copy](https://assets.mdrt.org/download/1d0b8a947c4f11f0b9761626a39ca69b)
- [VERIFIED-snippet] Requirements are set market by market; the "conversion factor" is used only to standardise local-currency production into USD for MDRT processing and "has no relationship to currency exchange rates". So THB thresholds cannot be derived from the THB/USD exchange rate. — [MDRT 2026 membership information](https://assets.mdrt.org/download/3af8200430fe11f0ba79fe135e3fbd63); [MDRT 2021 Conversion Factors](https://my.mdrt.org/Forms/2021_Conversion_Factors_English.pdf)

**2027 membership year (based on 2026 production)**
- [VERIFIED-snippet] "The 2027 commission requirement is USD 92,000." — [MDRT, Membership Information for the 2027 Million Dollar Round Table Based on 2026 production](https://assets.mdrt.org/download/a096e5cad48211f197217ee92a45f438)
- [VERIFIED-snippet] Risk-Protection test in the 2027 document: "the applicant must submit a minimum of USD 46,000 in commission from products listed in the Risk-Protection category" (= 50% of USD 92,000). — [MDRT 2027 membership information](https://assets.mdrt.org/download/a096e5cad48211f197217ee92a45f438)

**COT / TOT multiples**
- COT = production more than 3x MDRT; TOT = more than 6x MDRT (Thai source). — [AIA Thailand MDRT supplement](https://www.aia.co.th/content/dam/th/th/docs/mdrt/Supplement_AIA_vol2_Final.pdf); consistent with Hong Kong table (444,400 / 1,333,200 / 2,666,400 HKD commission) at [Bowtie](https://www.bowtie.com.hk/blog/en/insurance101/what-is-mdrt/)
- Applying the confirmed multiples to official USD commission: 2026 COT USD 261,000, TOT USD 522,000; 2027 COT USD 276,000, TOT USD 552,000 (arithmetic from the figures above, not quoted from the documents).

Unclear whether "2025" means membership year 2025 (2024 production) or 2025 production. — [wangpaan.com](https://www.wangpaan.com/content/16179/mdrt)

### Inferences
- The THB pattern in third-party sources (commission ≈ 30% of premium threshold; ~THB 0.6–0.7M commission / ~THB 1.9–2.2M premium) suggests the 2026 and 2027 Thai thresholds are in the same range, somewhat higher each year, but this must be confirmed from the official table.
- App design: store thresholds in a config table keyed by `membership_year` × `method` (commission / premium / income) × `level` (MDRT / COT / TOT), editable by the office admin, with source URL and "verified on" date. Never compute THB from FX.
- The progress tracker should count **calendar-year production (1 Jan–31 Dec)** toward the *next* membership year (2026 production → MDRT 2027), show % to MDRT / COT / TOT, required run-rate per remaining month, and three parallel meters (commission, premium, income) because an agent qualifies by any one method.
- Show an "Academy / Aspirant" tier under 100% (Academy = below MDRT line; 50% line matters for meeting attendance — see Q4).

### Gaps
- **Official Thailand THB figures for 2026 and 2027 (commission, premium, income; MDRT/COT/TOT) were not retrieved.** Source to read: mdrt.org/join/member-requirements (select Thailand) or the PDFs cited above. assets.mdrt.org / www.mdrt.org must be allowed in the session network settings to fetch them.
- 2027 USD premium and income-method figures not confirmed verbatim (only USD 92,000 commission is).
- 2026 USD income-method figure not retrieved.
- No Thai insurer page (AIA, MTL, KTAXA, Thai Life, FWD, Allianz Ayudhya) with a dated 2026/2027 THB table was found in search.

## Q2. Which products/credits count, minimum Risk-Protection requirement, ethics, persistency / compliance

### Takeaway
Qualification is production-based (commission, premium or income method) plus an ethics/good-standing commitment and dues; the documents for both 2026 and 2027 describe a 50% Risk-Protection minimum, though one 2026 extract says that rule was eliminated — a conflict that must be checked for the Thailand market. No MDRT-level persistency ratio requirement was found.

### Cited Findings
- [VERIFIED-snippet] 2026: half of the requirement must come from Risk-Protection products: USD 43,500 commission (or USD 87,000 premium). The document's worked example shows USD 43,499 of Risk-Protection commission is NOT eligible and USD 43,500 is. — [MDRT 2026 membership information](https://assets.mdrt.org/download/3af8200430fe11f0ba79fe135e3fbd63)
- [CONFLICT] A second 2026 MDRT document extract states MDRT "has eliminated the requirement that a minimum of 50% of an applicant's qualifying production come from Risk-Protection Credit or [Core credit]". This may be a market-specific version (the local-currency table is labelled "non-core"). — [MDRT 2026 membership information, alternate version](https://assets.mdrt.org/download/3c03651c30fe11f0a7cf9af9dbe5e106)
- [VERIFIED-snippet] 2027: Risk-Protection minimum USD 46,000 commission. — [MDRT 2027 membership information](https://assets.mdrt.org/download/a096e5cad48211f197217ee92a45f438)
- Three qualification methods exist: commission, premium, income (Thai MDRT table lists commission, income and premium for MDRT/COT/TOT). — [Lemon8 post describing MDRT Thailand 2027 table](https://www.lemon8-app.com/@athiwmagoeo/7591879217433657874?region=th); [MDRT member requirements page](https://www.mdrt.org/join/member-requirements/)
- Members must meet the production requirement, follow the MDRT Code of Ethics, pay annual dues and apply each year (membership is annual). — [luluinsure.my guide](https://luluinsure.my/en/guides/what-is-mdrt/) (third-party summary); dues in [MDRT 2027 membership information](https://assets.mdrt.org/download/a096e5cad48211f197217ee92a45f438)
- Applicants must belong to an MDRT-approved professional association; an older MDRT list names Thai Life Underwriters Association and GAMA International–Thailand for Thailand (2021 list — check current). — [MDRT approved professional associations (2021)](https://members.mdrt.org/globalassets/approved-professional-associations-for-web-02-04-21-1.pdf)
- Ethics screening applies across MDRT bodies: the MDRT Center requires agreeing to its Code of Ethics and having no existing ethics violation on file with MDRT, the Center or the Academy. — [MDRT Center FAQ](https://mdrtcenter.org/faq)
- Production is certified (a certifying letter from the company states commissions/premium credited). — [MDRT certifying letter document](https://assets.mdrt.org/download/b96b95c0459d11f09821b23e64b57eee)

### Inferences
- The tracker needs a second meter: "Risk-Protection share" (life/health/CI/riders vs savings/investment/other) with a 50% floor flag, switchable off if the Thai-market rule is confirmed removed.
- Because MDRT counts *paid and credited* production certified by the insurer, the app should distinguish submitted vs issued/paid cases and warn that only the insurer's certified figure counts.
- Persistency is not an MDRT global qualification rule in the sources found, but Thai insurers commonly attach persistency conditions to their own MDRT conventions/bonuses; an agency app should track persistency as an office KPI alongside MDRT progress rather than as an MDRT rule.

### Gaps
- Exact list of Risk-Protection vs Other product categories (and how Thai products such as unit-linked, endowment, health riders, PA, group are classified) not retrieved.
- Full text of the MDRT Code of Ethics not retrieved (unsourced here, so not listed).
- Whether MDRT imposes any persistency/lapse requirement, and whether Thai insurers require a persistency ratio before certifying production: no reliable source found.
- Whether the 50% Risk-Protection rule applies to Thailand for 2026/2027: unresolved conflict.

## Q3. Whole Person concept — the seven areas and self-assessment

### Takeaway
MDRT's Whole Person concept asks members to pursue balance across seven life areas — relationships, health, education, career, service, financial and spiritual — and MDRT offers a "balance quiz" for self-evaluation; balance does not mean equal weighting.

### Cited Findings
- The seven components are: relationships, career, health, service, financial, educational, spiritual. — [MDRT video: Unlocking greater career success through the MDRT Whole Person concept (2024)](https://www.mdrt.org/learn/2024/video/unlocking-greater-career-success-through-the-mdrt-whole-person-concept); [MDRT Whole Person Concept page](https://members.mdrt.org/about-MDRT/whole-person/)
- MDRT credits Dr. Mortimer Adler with presenting the philosophy to MDRT; a 2018 MDRT article says MDRT remains committed "almost 60 years" after he presented it (i.e. early 1960s). — [MDRT: Is your Whole Person plan in place? (2018)](https://www.mdrt.org/learn/2018/html/2018ampro-is-your-whole-person-plan-in-place)
- MDRT content describes the areas as spokes of a wheel, and states the Whole Person "isn't four or five or six equal parts" — emphasis shifts by life stage. — [MDRT podcast/article: Get a life (2025)](https://www.mdrt.org/learn/2025/html/get-a-life); [How to embrace the Whole Person concept (2024)](https://www.mdrt.org/learn/2024/audio/how-to-embrace-the-whole-person-concept)
- MDRT's site offers a "balance quiz" with questions to "evaluate yourself as a Whole Person". — [MDRT Whole Person Concept page](https://members.mdrt.org/about-MDRT/whole-person/)
- Related MDRT material: "Creating balance through MDRT's Whole Person concept" (Round the Table, 2017). — [MDRT Round the Table 2017](https://www.mdrt.org/learn/2017/html/rtt201705-creating-balance-through-mdrts-whole-person-concept)

### Inferences
- Working definitions for app copy (my paraphrase — official wording was not retrievable, verify against the MDRT page before publishing): **Relationships** – time and quality of connection with family, friends, colleagues; **Health** – physical and mental well-being, exercise, rest, check-ups; **Education** – continuous learning, professional and personal; **Career** – professional growth, client service quality, productivity; **Service** – giving back to community, industry, charity; **Financial** – own financial plan, protection, savings, living within means; **Spiritual** – values, purpose, faith or inner peace.
- Check-in design: a monthly or quarterly 7-spoke wheel, self-rating 1–10 per area, one small commitment per low-scoring area, trend over time; private to the agent by default with opt-in sharing to mentor/manager (health, spiritual and family items are sensitive personal data under Thailand's PDPA). Do not rank agents on Whole Person scores.
- Avoid copying MDRT's quiz questions or logo; "Whole Person" and MDRT marks are MDRT's intellectual property — describe the feature as "inspired by" unless the office has permission.

### Gaps
- Official one-line definitions of each of the seven areas and the exact balance-quiz questions/scoring were not retrieved (members.mdrt.org blocked).
- Year of Adler's original presentation: not quoted directly.

## Q4. MDRT Academy, Mentoring Program, MDRT Global Services / MDRT Center for Field Leadership

### Takeaway
MDRT Academy is the pre-MDRT association for advisors below the MDRT line; the Mentoring Program pairs a current MDRT member (mentor) with an aspirant (mentee), with 50% of the MDRT requirement as the key aspirant milestone for meeting attendance; "MDRT Global Services" was renamed the MDRT Center for Field Leadership on 1 Nov 2023 and is for field/home-office leaders with no production requirement.

### Cited Findings
**MDRT Academy**
- For those who do not qualify for MDRT; 2027 band is USD 0 to USD 91,999 eligible commission; membership is one year at a time. — [MDRT Academy 2027 requirements](https://app.mdrtacademy.org/public/documents/membership_requirements_en)
- 2026 Academy document (US example) put the band at USD 0 to USD 150,999 of eligible premium paid. — [MDRT local-currency/Academy 2026 document](https://assets.mdrt.org/download/4e6c5526d6c911f0aa5ce25980888793)
- The MDRT Academy is not available in Mainland China, Japan and Korea (so it is available in Thailand by implication, not explicitly confirmed). — [2026 Academy Eligibility Requirements](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/625972d816f95a3de5e6981d/6931dce529a0b92a26fd97b9_2026_Academy_Eligibility_Requirements_ENG.pdf)

**Mentoring Program**
- Advisors who want to take part in the Mentoring Program and/or attend an MDRT meeting must join the MDRT Academy; must be a current Academy member or enrolled and connected in the Mentoring Program by 31 March of the meeting year. — [2026 Academy Eligibility Requirements](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/625972d816f95a3de5e6981d/6931dce529a0b92a26fd97b9_2026_Academy_Eligibility_Requirements_ENG.pdf)
- Meeting-attendance threshold for aspirants: production of 50% of the meeting year's MDRT requirement (commission, premium or income); max two meetings in total, one per year; a mentee's mentor must be registered for the same meeting. — [2026 Academy Eligibility Requirements](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/625972d816f95a3de5e6981d/6931dce529a0b92a26fd97b9_2026_Academy_Eligibility_Requirements_ENG.pdf)
- Mentors must be current MDRT members willing to commit to the relationship; all members are encouraged to mentor (contact mentoring@mdrt.org). — [MDRT Mentorship page](https://members.mdrt.org/connect/mentorship/)
- Legacy FAQ: no production requirement to enrol as a mentee, enrolment open year-round; non-member mentees must report year-end production to MDRT annually. — [MDRT legacy mentorship page](https://legacy.mdrt.org/mentorship/)
- Program described as a 12-month structure with milestones and tracked progress, plus best-practice training for mentor and mentee. — [Insurance Portal: MDRT revamps mentoring program](https://insurance-portal.ca/article/mdrt-revamps-mentoring-program/); [MDRT Mentoring 101 brochure "The Art of Mentoring"](https://www.imdrt.org/mentoring/MDRT_Mentoring_101_Brochure.pdf)
- Superseded older rule: a 75% production tier allowed a second meeting attendance (2017–2018 sources). — [2017 blog on aspirant attendance](http://maritimewealth.blogspot.com/2017/02/attend-mdrt-as-aspirant-or-manager.html)

**MDRT Global Services → MDRT Center for Field Leadership**
- MDRT Global Services was renamed MDRT Center for Field Leadership effective 1 November 2023. — [ACN Newswire press release](https://en.acnnewswire.com/press-release/english/87361/mdrt-global-services-rebrands-as-mdrt-center-for-field-leadership)
- Eligibility: field or home-office leader in financial services (agency director, general agent, managing director, sales manager, recruiter, sales support specialist, etc.); must agree to the Center's Code of Ethics with no ethics violation on file; no sales/commission numbers required; membership annual, open enrolment 1 Nov–1 Apr (late fee USD 200); dues listed as USD 600/year (one page shows USD 300 without clear context). — [MDRT Center FAQ](https://mdrtcenter.org/faq)
- Academy members who are also leaders may join the Center as well. — [MDRT Center FAQ](https://mdrtcenter.org/faq)
- Culture of Excellence Awards: example criterion — a manager with a team of 10 advisors would need 8 who were 2025 MDRT or 2025 MDRT Academy members (i.e. 80%). — [MDRT Center awards FAQ](https://www.mdrtcenter.org/awards-faq); [Culture of Excellence Awards](https://mdrtcenter.org/culture-of-excellence-awards)
- MDRT, MDRT Academy and MDRT Center are presented as a "family of brands". — [MDRT Family of Brands](https://www.mdrt.org/family-of-brands)

### Inferences
- App mentoring module: mentor (qualified MDRT in the office) ↔ aspirant pairs, 12-month plan, monthly milestone check-ins, aspirant bands at 25% / 50% (meeting-eligibility line) / 75% / 100% of the MDRT requirement, and a year-end production report.
- Office dashboard KPI for the GA/manager: "% of agents who are MDRT or Academy members" (mirrors the 80% Culture of Excellence example), number of first-time MDRT qualifiers, repeat qualifiers (longevity).
- The office manager is the natural MDRT Center member; agents below the line are natural Academy members — the app can show each person which body fits them.

### Gaps
- Exact Culture of Excellence award tiers and whether 80% is the general rule or just the worked example.
- Official current aspirant "typical targets" beyond the 50% meeting threshold; MDRT Academy dues and Thai availability not confirmed verbatim.

## Q6. Activity disciplines used by MDRT members — official vs general industry practice

### Takeaway
No MDRT-official activity-points or ratio system was found; MDRT's official standards are production, ethics and the Whole Person philosophy, while activity ratios and card systems are general industry practice shared by members and insurers.

### Cited Findings
- The "Granum system" is also known as the Original One Card System (OCS) — referenced as an advisor prospecting/record-keeping method in an industry forum, not as an MDRT standard. — [WealthManagement.com forum](https://www.wealthmanagement.com/forum_print/prospecting-marketing-and-selling/keep-score)
- Thai agent blogs break the MDRT premium goal into case-count/case-size paths (e.g. FYP THB 2,000,000 split by number of cases and average premium) — agency practice, not MDRT rule. — [iagencyaia.com](https://iagencyaia.com/blog/road-to-mdrt-idea/)
- Thai insurers run their own pre-MDRT pipelines (e.g. AIA "Pre MDRT Club" for first-timers). — [Hoonsmart](https://hoonsmart.com/archives/405664)

### Inferences
- The app should label activity features as "office discipline" (configurable): weekly activity points, funnel ratios (prospects → appointments → presentations → closed cases), average case size; then back-solve from the MDRT gap: remaining premium ÷ average case size = cases needed → × the agent's own measured ratios = appointments and prospects needed per week.
- Use each agent's own historical conversion ratios rather than a fixed "10-3-1", which is a rule of thumb.

### Gaps
- No citable source was retrieved for: the origin of "10-3-1" (commonly attributed to Al Granum of Northwestern Mutual and his One Card System, based on long-term activity records — background knowledge, unsourced here); weekly points systems (e.g. "30 points a week"/"100-point week" variants are insurer or trainer practices — unsourced here). None should be presented in the app as MDRT-official.
- MDRT's own member-shared practice-management content (mdrt.org/learn) could not be searched in depth.
