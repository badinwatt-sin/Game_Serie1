# GAMA (GAMA International / GAMA Global / Finseca / GAMA Thailand) — field-leadership framework, awards and agency-building practices

> **Method caveat (read first).** Research date: 2026-11-24. In this session direct page fetches were blocked for every host tried (gamaglobal.org, gama.com.hk, finseca.org, advocis.ca, thinkadvisor.com, mitihoon.com, wangpaan.com — all outside the session's network allowlist). ## 1. Organisational status of GAMA / GAMA Global / Finseca (2026) and presence in Asia and Thailand

### Takeaway
GAMA International (founded 1952) merged with AALU to form Finseca in 2020; its international arm continues under the name **GAMA Global**, which still runs the awards programme and LAMP, and whose CEO is also Finseca's president. Thailand has an active chapter, **GAMA Thailand** (สมาคมผู้บริหารธุรกิจประกันชีวิตและที่ปรึกษาการเงิน), which runs a large annual "GAMA Awards" ceremony (hundreds of Thai IMA/FLA/ERA qualifiers per year).

### Cited Findings

- AALU and GAMA International announced the intention to merge on 29 July 2019; members approved the new organisation on 18 February 2020. — [Finseca announcement](https://www.finseca.org/aalu-gama-to-build-new-organization-representing-the-financial-security-profession-2/)

- Chapter/country counts conflict between sources: "9 chapters with membership in 22 countries" — [Finseca: GAMA Global](https://www.finseca.org/international/), [GAMA Global LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/gama-global); versus "members in over 25 countries … 13 local chapters" — [GAMA Global homepage](https://gamaglobal.org/). ### Inferences
- For a Thai agency, "GAMA" in practice means GAMA Thailand's annual awards (IMA / FLA / ERA are the three that almost all Thai leaders chase); these are the natural "external badge" targets to model in the app for directors (IMA), unit managers (FLA) and anyone recruiting (ERA).
- Thai insurers use GAMA counts in PR, so an owner-level screen showing "number of GAMA qualifiers in my office, by level" mirrors how the industry already reports success.
- The Thai tier counts show a broad base (Bronze/Silver dominate FLA: 723 of 784), so tiered progress bars with a reachable first tier are realistic motivators.

### Gaps
- GAMA Thailand's membership count, fee, and founding year were not found.
- Which chapter/country count (9/22 vs 13/25+) is current is unresolved.
- No information found on a 2026 LAMP edition or on LAMP Asia dates/locations.
- Which production year the 727/784 Thai counts refer to (event year 2024, presumably 2023 production) is not confirmed.

## 2. GAMA management awards — criteria, formulae, levels (latest: 2027 awards recognising 2026 production)

### Takeaway
The awards are almost entirely **team first-year commission (FYC) or first-year premium thresholds**, with a minimum number of supervised full-time agents, plus a separate recruiting award counting new non-industry recruits still active at year end; I found **no persistency or agent-retention threshold in IMA/FLA/ERA** extracts (only an optional "renewal product percentages" benchmark in the US FLA form). The Agency Builder, Career Development and National Management Award criteria could not be found in current GAMA Global material.

### Cited Findings
**General rules (GAMA Global brochure, 2027 awards)**
- Production period 1 January–31 December 2026; only new first-year commissions or premiums count; for the Ordinary/Traditional category life insurance must be at least 50% of total commissions/premiums; firms measuring gross dealer concession (GDC) instead of FYC should halve their figures; all qualifiers must be current GAMA Global members. — [2027 GAMA Global Awards Brochure](https://gamaglobal.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2027-gama-global-awards-brochure.pdf); [GAMA Global Awards page](https://gamaglobal.org/awards/)
- Deadlines: 28 February 2027 for recognition at GAMA Day chapter events and LAMP; 1 September 2027 for the Nov/Dec Journal edition. — [2027 brochure](https://gamaglobal.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2027-gama-global-awards-brochure.pdf). GAMAHK lists its own 2027 deadline as "TBD"; its 2026 deadline was 27 March 2026. — [GAMAHK 2027 criteria](https://www.gama.com.hk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/GAMA-Global-Awards-Criteria-2027.pdf); [GAMAHK 2026 criteria](https://www.gama.com.hk/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/C1-GAMA-Global-Awards-Criteria-2026-v2601.pdf)

**International Management Award (IMA)**
- Recognises first-line managers (heads of agencies/firms) and frontline/second-/middle-line managers; only FYC paid to the applicant and his/her **direct and indirect** reports counts. — [GAMA Global Awards page](https://gamaglobal.org/awards/); [2026 brochure](https://gamaglobal.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/2026-gama-global-awards-brochure.pdf)
- Must "personally supervise at least 8 full-time career agents under direct agency as of 31st December 2026". — [GAMAHK 2027 criteria](https://www.gama.com.hk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/GAMA-Global-Awards-Criteria-2027.pdf) **[extract, cross-checked]**
- 2027 IMA levels, team FYC in USD (HKD equivalent): Bronze 300,000 (1,395,000); Silver 600,000 (2,790,000); Gold 1,200,000 (5,580,000); Platinum 1,800,000 (8,370,000); Diamond 2,500,000 (11,625,000); Titanium 4,000,000 (18,600,000). HK application fee about HK$1,980 per level. — [GAMAHK 2027 criteria](https://www.gama.com.hk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/GAMA-Global-Awards-Criteria-2027.pdf) **[extract, cross-checked; all HKD figures equal USD x 4.65]**
- The US brochure's worked example: "USD/ICE commission of 2,500,000 you qualify for the IMA Diamond Award. With a USD/ICE premium of 3,600,000 you qualify for the IMA Platinum Award." — [2027 brochure](https://gamaglobal.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2027-gama-global-awards-brochure.pdf)
- Qualifying for the MAA automatically certifies the applicant for the IMA at one level with no further form or fee. — [GAMAHK 2026 criteria](https://www.gama.com.hk/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/C1-GAMA-Global-Awards-Criteria-2026-v2601.pdf)

**Frontline Leader Award (FLA)**
- For frontline managers (unit, division, district managers with direct responsibility for part of an agency); must be sales/unit managers (frontline or second-line); only individual FYC paid to the applicant and his/her **direct reports** counts; must personally supervise full-time career agents under direct team as of 31 December 2026; fee USD 100. — [2027 brochure](https://gamaglobal.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2027-gama-global-awards-brochure.pdf); [GAMA Global Awards page](https://gamaglobal.org/awards/)
- US form: a matrix completed from the production of each agent/representative; Benchmark 1 = average life premium sold; applicant may enter Benchmark 2A (new products sold) or 2B (renewal product percentages); "Your award level will be determined by the highest benchmark met." Dollar/percentage values of the benchmarks were not visible. — [2027 brochure](https://gamaglobal.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2027-gama-global-awards-brochure.pdf)
- GAMAHK 2027 FLA levels, team FYC in USD (HKD): Bronze 140,000 (651,000); Silver 280,000 (1,302,000); Gold 560,000 (2,604,000); Platinum 1,000,000 (4,650,000); Diamond 1,500,000 (6,975,000); fees HK$1,280–1,480. — [GAMAHK 2027 criteria](https://www.gama.com.hk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/GAMA-Global-Awards-Criteria-2027.pdf) **[extract; Bronze–Platinum from one query, Diamond from another; HKD ratio consistent]**
- Older rule (2023 criteria): "Personally supervise at least 5 full-time career agents under direct team/supervision"; only FYC of applicant and directly supervised agents counted. Not confirmed for 2027. — [GAMAHK 2023 criteria](https://www.gama.com.hk/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/GAMA-Awards-Criteria-2023-1107-International-with-ERA-Logo.pdf)

**Excellence Recruitment Award (ERA)** (used by Asian chapters incl. Thailand and Hong Kong)
- Count only new recruits under the applicant's direct agency; count only non-industry ("non-industrial") recruits; recruits must be in force at 31 December of the production year (31 Dec 2025 for 2026 awards; 31 Dec 2026 for 2027). — [GAMAHK 2026 criteria](https://www.gama.com.hk/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/C1-GAMA-Global-Awards-Criteria-2026-v2601.pdf); [GAMAHK 2027 criteria](https://www.gama.com.hk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/GAMA-Global-Awards-Criteria-2027.pdf)
- Minimum active new recruits: Gold 4, Platinum 6, Diamond 10 (HK fee HK$800). — [GAMAHK 2027 criteria](https://www.gama.com.hk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/GAMA-Global-Awards-Criteria-2027.pdf) **[extract; one query attributed these numbers to ERA, another to a recruitment condition attached to FLA/IMA levels — attribution ambiguous]**
- Thailand awards ERA at Bronze and Silver levels too (see section 1 counts). — [Siamrath](https://siamrath.co.th/n/558417)

**Master Agency Award (MAA) / Master Firm Award (MFA) / Master Multiline Award (MMA)**
- MAA is for agency heads; only a main/central office representing the whole firm qualifies; fee USD 300. — [2027 brochure](https://gamaglobal.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2027-gama-global-awards-brochure.pdf)
- Threshold: at least USD 7,800,000 in commissions or USD 15,600,000 in premiums in the previous year (same wording shown for Master Firm Award). — [GAMA Global Awards page](https://gamaglobal.org/awards/) **[extract, single]**
- MMA: minimum USD 300,000 gross life and annuity premiums and USD 10,000,000 gross P&C premiums. — [2027 brochure](https://gamaglobal.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2027-gama-global-awards-brochure.pdf) **[extract, single]**

**Thai-specific description**
- A Thai agency-recruiting site describes tiers "Seat of Honor (10+ MDRT), Seat of Glory (6–9 MDRT), Seat of Distinction (3–5 MDRT)" and attributes them to IMA. — [wangpaan](https://www.wangpaan.com/gama) **[extract, single; attribution doubtful — see Inferences]**

### Inferences
- Premium thresholds appear to be exactly 2x the commission thresholds (IMA Platinum 1.8M commission vs 3.6M premium example; MAA 7.8M vs 15.6M). This is my reading of the pattern, not a stated rule.
- The "Seat of Honor/Glory/Distinction by number of MDRT qualifiers in the team" tiers most likely belong to the Circle of Excellence (COE) award rather than IMA, since IMA tiers are metal-named FYC levels in every other source. Unverified.
- What the awards measure, mapped to app scorecards:
  - **Director scorecard (IMA logic):** total FYC of whole downline (direct + indirect), gate = at least 8 directly supervised full-time agents at 31 Dec. Show progress to next tier (300k / 600k / 1.2M / 1.8M / 2.5M / 4.0M USD).
  - **Unit-manager scorecard (FLA logic):** FYC of self + direct reports only; tiers 140k / 280k / 560k / 1.0M / 1.5M USD (HK chapter values); plus per-agent averages (US benchmark style: average premium per agent, policies/new products per agent).
  - **Recruiting scorecard (ERA logic):** count of new, non-industry, directly recruited agents who are still active/in force on 31 Dec; milestones 4 / 6 / 10.
  - **Owner scorecard (MAA/COE logic):** whole-office FYC/premium and number of MDRT qualifiers.
- Because the awards themselves do not test persistency or 13-month agent retention, an app should add those as separate health metrics (from LIMRA-style measures) rather than assume GAMA covers them.
- Thresholds are USD; the Thai chapter's baht conversions/exchange rate were not found, so the app should store tier thresholds as editable configuration per award year, not constants.

### Gaps
- **Agency Builder Award / Agency Achievement Award, Career Development Award, National Management Award (NMA), "First in Class":** no criteria found in any 2024–2027 GAMA Global or GAMAHK extract. These were historic GAMA International (US) awards; whether they still exist in 2026 is unconfirmed, and I will not state their criteria from memory.
- RSA and COE definitions and thresholds not found (COE = "Circle of Excellence Awards" per Thai press; RSA expansion not seen).
- US FLA benchmark values (average life premium per agent, products sold, renewal %) not visible.
- GAMA Thailand's own criteria document (baht thresholds, exchange rate, fees, minimum agent counts, ERA Bronze/Silver numbers) not retrieved; gamathailand.org must be read directly.
- No persistency/retention requirement was seen, but absence in extracts is not proof of absence in the full PDFs.
- "Front Line Excellence" as a separate award name: not found; the current name is Frontline Leader Award.

## 3. Leadership competency models and programmes (ELM, LAMP, GAMA Foundation research, master-agency practices)

### Takeaway
GAMA's teaching is organised around a people pipeline — **find the right people, build the right people, keep the right people, build a high-performance culture** — delivered through the Field Leadership Series / Masters of Retention workshops and the Essentials of Leadership and Management course (recruiting process, development process, accountability process). The specific "Building the Master Agency / 10 building blocks" content could not be retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- Field Leadership Series (FLS) / "Masters of Retention": four workshops covering recruiting and selection, new advisor success, retention, and high-performance culture. — [GAMA Global Training](https://gamaglobal.org/training/)
- "Finding the Right People" workshop focuses on primary and secondary recruiting sources and screening tools to assess candidates' fit, willingness and ability to perform; the selection workshop aligns the selection process with an "ideal candidate profile" so that it "results in selecting candidates that you will retain". — [GAMA Global Training](https://gamaglobal.org/training/)
- The high-performance culture workshop "identifies the 10 best practices that top-performing leaders use to create a high-performance culture in their agencies and firms". — [GAMA Global Training](https://gamaglobal.org/training/)
- A "Building the Right People" one-pager (2017) exists as part of the series (finding / building / keeping the right people); intended audience includes agency and general-agency managers and trainers. — [Finseca-hosted one-pager](https://www.finseca.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/OnePager_BuildingRightPeople_2017.pdf); [insurancedaily.gr](https://insurancedaily.gr/gama-anaptyxi-epicheirimatikis-skepsis)
- Essentials of Leadership and Management (ELM) is "Course 1" of the GAMA Leadership and Management Proficiency (LMP) programme for field leaders; it includes building a plan for recruiting and selection. GAMAcademy lists modules on the recruiting process, the development process, and the accountability process. — [GAMA Global Training](https://gamaglobal.org/training/); [GAMAcademy courses](https://hello.fsednet.com/gamacademy/courses-and-pricing/)
- Member benefits include self-paced e-learning on recruiting, retention, selection and team-building culture. — [GAMA Global Member Benefits](https://gamaglobal.org/member-benefits/)
- GAMA Foundation "Performance Profile Series: Creating a High-Impact Recruiting Culture" names 10 highest-impact best practices; the first three: (1) cultivate a recruiting mindset in the agency/firm, (2) focus on warm-source recruiting, (3) apply a rigorous and consistent approach to selection; the fourth moves to a high-expectations culture (setting high expectations for new agents and advisors). — [GAMA Foundation report, Advocis-hosted PDF](https://www.advocis.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/GAMA-Creating-a-High-Impact-Recruiting-Culture.pdf)
- A 2005 trade article covers "two playbooks for building a successful agency" in connection with GAMA's master-agency research (content not retrievable). — [ThinkAdvisor, 2005-03-16](https://www.thinkadvisor.com/2005/03/16/two-playbooks-for-building-a-successful-agency/)
- GAMA Foundation publishes Retention eBooks, e.g. "Retention Volume One: Focusing New Agents on Revenue-Producing Activities" (multiline edition, 2016). — [GAMA Foundation Retention eBooks](https://www.gamafoundation.org/GAMAF/Research/eBooks/Retention/gamaf/Research/Retention_eBooks.aspx?hkey=901c3574-1267-4263-af19-0ac7c076b044); [Finseca-hosted PDF](https://www.finseca.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Retention-SeriesAgents_Vol1-Multiline-Final-11-10-161.pdf)

### Inferences
- A usable competency skeleton for the app's leader-development path, derived from the programme structure: (1) Recruiting (sourcing, warm sources), (2) Selection (ideal candidate profile, consistent screening steps), (3) New-advisor development/onboarding, (4) Accountability/performance management, (5) Retention, (6) Culture. Succession planning was not seen in any extract.
- "Recruiting process – development process – accountability process" maps cleanly onto three app modules: recruiting pipeline, training/joint-work tracker, activity & results accountability.

### Gaps
- "Building the Master Agency" and any "10 building blocks/core practices of master agencies": existence as a GAMA Foundation title and its contents not confirmed.
- Items 5–10 of the 10 best practices not retrieved.
- No explicit GAMA competency model document, and no GAMA material on succession planning, was found.
- LAMP agenda/curriculum content not retrieved.

## 4. Published best practices: recruiting pipelines, onboarding, joint work/coaching, activity management, second-line leaders

### Takeaway
The retrievable GAMA guidance is directional rather than procedural: build a recruiting mindset across the whole agency, favour warm-source (referral/nomination) recruits, run one rigorous and consistent selection process for every candidate, set high expectations from day one, and focus new agents on revenue-producing activities. Detailed GAMA procedures for joint work, coaching cadence and second-line leader development were not retrievable.

### Cited Findings
- Top-three recruiting practices: recruiting mindset in the agency, warm-source recruiting, rigorous and consistent selection; then setting high expectations for new agents and advisors. — [GAMA Foundation, Creating a High-Impact Recruiting Culture](https://www.advocis.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/GAMA-Creating-a-High-Impact-Recruiting-Culture.pdf)
- Recruiting sources are split into primary and secondary; screening assesses fit, willingness and ability. — [GAMA Global Training](https://gamaglobal.org/training/)
- Retention guidance is framed around focusing new agents on revenue-producing activities. — [GAMA Foundation Retention Vol. 1](https://www.finseca.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Retention-SeriesAgents_Vol1-Multiline-Final-11-10-161.pdf)
- 45% of field leaders responding to a GAMA Foundation survey believed it typically takes more than 12 months for new recruits to become productive (c. 2016). — [GAMA Foundation Retention Vol. 1](https://www.finseca.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Retention-SeriesAgents_Vol1-Multiline-Final-11-10-161.pdf)
- Practitioner view (not GAMA): warm-source recruiting is described as one of the most consistently validated predictors of better retention and performance versus cold-sourced hires. — [Chris Gee, LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/recruitment-best-practices-checklist-chris-gee)
- Award design itself rewards non-industry recruits who are still active at year end, recruited directly by the applicant. — [GAMAHK 2026 criteria](https://www.gama.com.hk/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/C1-GAMA-Global-Awards-Criteria-2026-v2601.pdf)

### Inferences (app feature implications)
- **Recruiting pipeline:** tag every candidate with source type (warm: referral/nominator/client/personal vs cold: job board/walk-in) and whether from the industry; fixed, identical stage checklist for all candidates (profile fit, willingness, ability, job sample/expectations talk); report conversion by source.
- **Recruiting mindset:** let every agent (not only managers) submit nominations and see credit; leaderboard of nominators.
- **Onboarding:** an expectations contract at contracting (activity and production minimums for the first 90 days / 12 months) with weekly tracking of revenue-producing activities (appointments set, fact-finds, closes) rather than hours.
- **Second-line leaders:** an FLA-style personal scoreboard for unit managers (own + direct-report FYC, number of full-time agents, active new recruits) is the clearest externally recognised ladder to IMA.
- **Recognition:** show in-app GAMA tier badges and "distance to next tier", and an "active at 31 Dec" countdown for new recruits, since that is the ERA counting date.

### Gaps
- No GAMA-published joint-work ratios, coaching cadence, PEP/activity-point systems, or second-line leader development curriculum were retrievable. These should be sourced from LIMRA/MDRT notes rather than attributed to GAMA.

## 5. Concrete benchmarks (recruit-to-hire ratios, retention targets, span of control)

### Takeaway
Very few hard GAMA benchmarks were retrievable; the only numeric ones are award gates (8 direct full-time agents for IMA; 4/6/10 active new recruits) and old retention statistics (43% four-year retention, multiline agents, 2014).

### Cited Findings
- Four-year retention rate for multiline agents was 43% in 2014 (LIMRA data cited by GAMA Foundation). — [GAMA Foundation Retention Vol. 1 (2016)](https://www.finseca.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Retention-SeriesAgents_Vol1-Multiline-Final-11-10-161.pdf)
- Only about 12% (one in eight) of GAMA member respondents believed their agencies were extremely effective at keeping agents (c. 2016). — [same source](https://www.finseca.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Retention-SeriesAgents_Vol1-Multiline-Final-11-10-161.pdf)
- 45% of field leaders said new recruits typically take more than 12 months to become productive (c. 2016). — [same source](https://www.finseca.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Retention-SeriesAgents_Vol1-Multiline-Final-11-10-161.pdf)
- Minimum team size gates: IMA at least 8 directly supervised full-time career agents (2027); FLA at least 5 (2023 criteria, unconfirmed for 2027). — [GAMAHK 2027](https://www.gama.com.hk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/GAMA-Global-Awards-Criteria-2027.pdf); [GAMAHK 2023](https://www.gama.com.hk/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/GAMA-Awards-Criteria-2023-1107-International-with-ERA-Logo.pdf)
- Recruiting gates: 4 / 6 / 10 active non-industry new recruits per year for Gold / Platinum / Diamond. — [GAMAHK 2027](https://www.gama.com.hk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/GAMA-Global-Awards-Criteria-2027.pdf)

### Inferences
- Implied productivity floors from the award tiers: IMA Bronze with the minimum 8 agents equals 37,500 USD FYC per agent per year (300,000 / 8); FLA Bronze with 5 agents equals 28,000 USD per agent (140,000 / 5). These are arithmetic illustrations, not GAMA-published per-agent benchmarks.
- The multiline retention figure (43%) is not a life-agency figure and is 12 years old; do not use it as a Thai target.
