## 1. Thailand regulatory learning requirements for life-insurance agents (OIC / คปภ.)

### Takeaway
The training ladder used by the Thai Life Assurance Association (TLAA) in its 2568 (2025) schedule is: 6 hours to apply for the agent licence, 6 hours for each of the 1st, 2nd and 3rd renewals, and 30 hours for the 4th renewal onward (with minimum hours per "Pillar"). Licence validity periods for individual agents, the pass mark for the exam, and the exact hours/IC rules for Unit-Linked and Universal Life could not be confirmed from primary OIC/SEC text and must be checked before publishing.

### Cited Findings

**Pre-licence training and renewal hours (source year 2568 / 2025, TLAA schedules, not the OIC notification itself)**

Year of source: 2568 (2025). — [TLAA training plan ขอรับ ตช1 ตช2 ตช3 ตช4 ปี68](https://www.tlaa.org/download/file/แผนการจัดอบรม%20ขอรับ%20ตช1%20ตช2%20ตช3%20และตช4%20ปี68.pdf)

- [V-extract] Thai press reported OIC plans to tighten sales rules, including a ban on re-contacting a customer for 6 months after a refusal. Status (proposed vs. in force) not confirmed. — [Prachachat](https://www.prachachat.net/finance/news-686256)

### Inferences
- The regulatory spine for a roadmap, on current extract-level evidence: pre-contract = 6-hour OIC course + exam; end of licence year 1 = 6h (renewal 1); year 2 = 6h (renewal 2); year 3 = 6h (renewal 3); thereafter 30h per renewal cycle with Pillar minimums. The 24-month mark therefore coincides with the second renewal.
- The 30-hour requirement is described as "accumulated" (TII), which suggests it can be built up across the licence period rather than taken in one block; an office could spread it over the leader-track years. This is an inference from wording, not a confirmed rule.
- Because renewal 3 onward runs on a longer cycle for juristic brokers, the same 1-1-1-5-year pattern probably applies to individual agents, but that is an inference only (see Gaps).
- Unit-linked selling is a separate gate with two regulators (OIC training/registration plus SEC IC licence), so it suits a milestone after basic validation, not the first 90 days.

### Gaps
- **Licence validity for individual agents [UNVERIFIED]:** researcher background knowledge (not confirmed by any extract here) is that under the Life Insurance Act B.E. 2535 the licence is valid 1 year, the first two renewals are 1 year each, and from the third renewal the licence is valid 5 years. No extract confirmed the section number or wording. Check the Act (Section 72 is the commonly cited section, unconfirmed) on oic.or.th.
- **OIC notification name, number and year** that sets the 6/6/6/6/30-hour scheme and the Pillar 1/2/3 structure: not found. Whether a notification issued in 2568-2569 (2025-2026) changed the hours is unknown; the latest evidence is the TLAA 2568 schedule.
- **Pillar 2 minimum hours and the definition of each Pillar:** not visible.
- **Exam format** (number of questions, pass mark, any separate ethics threshold): not confirmed. A 2025 blog mentions 100 questions / 200 points for an exam at the TII centre without clearly identifying it as the life-agent exam.
- **Whether the 6-hour pre-licence course must precede the exam** and any validity window for the exam result: not confirmed.
- **Unit-Linked/Universal Life hours:** the OIC-prescribed hours are not confirmed (TLAA's own course is 6 hours for unit-linked). Whether Universal Life needs only OIC training while Unit-Linked additionally needs an SEC IC licence [UNVERIFIED; researcher background knowledge says unit-linked sellers need an SEC IC licence, UL does not] must be checked with OIC/SEC. Current SEC exam paper (P1 vs. others) and IC renewal cycle/hours (TLAA's 15-hour IC renewal course suggests 15 hours, unconfirmed as the rule) also need checking.
- Continuing-education rules beyond renewal hours: none found.

## 2. How major insurers in Thailand/Asia stage new-agent development

### Takeaway
The common Asian pattern is a 12-month structured new-agent programme with an allowance or bonus tied to validation targets, preceded by a 90-day orientation block; AIA markets show this most clearly. For Thailand, only AIA and FWD programme names were confirmed; no curriculum detail was found for Muang Thai Life, Thai Life, Krungthai-AXA, Allianz Ayudhya, Prudential Thailand or Manulife.

### Cited Findings

**Thailand**
- [V-extract] AIA Thailand: recruits to the Financial Adviser (FA) programme go through a paid sales-training regimen of 9 to 12 months (article circa 2017). — [Bangkok Post](https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/1335431/aia-thailand-to-boost-financial-advisers)
- [V-extract] AIA Thailand "FA 2.0" described as a compulsory 12-month training course (2018). — [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/30353075)
- [V-extract] AIA Thailand launched "FA 4.0: The Ultimate Success" in January 2026, featuring a Career Achievement Bonus and AI Role Play for sales/advisory training. Allowance and validation terms not shown. — [Nation Thailand PR](https://www.nationthailand.com/pr-news/pr-news/40061125)
- [V-extract] An AIA Thailand agency-unit site states new agents must complete at least 9 e-learning courses within 6 months of receiving their AIA agent code (unit site, not AIA corporate; undated). — [iAgencyAIA new agent preparation](https://www.iagencyaia.com/new-agent-preparation)
- [V-extract] FWD Thailand "Agency Year Plan 2026" centres on "FWD FutureMe": agents hold the FutureMe role in their first year with quarterly coaching, positioned as a systematic programme to become professional advisers within one year. — [Kaohoon International](https://www.kaohooninternational.com/markets/576014)
- [V-extract] FWD partnered with INSEAD on a programme for agency leaders (Thailand press, circa 2019). — [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/noname/30372622)
- [V-extract] Third-party provider Wealthivities sells a 90-day new-agent course covering insurance basics, needs analysis, product presentation and closing (not an insurer programme). — [Wealthivities](https://wealthivities.co.th/courses/wealthivities-agent/)

**Other Asian markets (pattern evidence, not Thai rules)**
- [V-extract] AIA Singapore: 12-month intensive training programme with a monthly allowance tied to sales validation targets ("EPS" scheme). — [AIA Singapore recruitment brochure](https://aia.com.sg/content/dam/sg-wise/en/docs/about-aia/careers/aia-recruitment-brochure.pdf)
- [V-extract] AIA Philippines Premier Agency Recruitment Program: 12-month route with monthly activity payout and quarterly milestone bonus. — [AIA Philippines](https://www.aia.com.ph/en/financialadvisor/explore-articles/articles/starting-strong-with-aia)
- [V-extract] AIA Malaysia Elite Academy: monthly bonuses subject to validation requirements. — [AIA Malaysia](https://www.aia.com.my/content/dam/my/en/images/insuranceagent/elite-academy/AIA_ELITE_PREMIER_LEADER_FINAL.pdf)
- [V-extract] Prudential Hong Kong: "Fast Start" series comprising orientation, selling skills, product knowledge, digital systems and a "90 Days Orientation Program"; PRUExpert online system with 100+ classes; an accredited diploma under the HK Qualifications Framework. — [Prudential HK training support](https://www.prudential.com.hk/jsp/prurecruit/en/Ideal-Platform/Training-Support/index.jsp); [Prudential HK](https://www.prudential.com.hk/en/be-a-prudential-agent)

- [V-extract] Prudential Singapore: the first two years are described as the crucial period in which new joiners decide whether the career suits them. — [The Edge Singapore](https://testing.dev.theedgesingapore.com/news/company-news/prudential-ramps-hiring-bolsters-support-newcomers-start-careers-financial)

### Inferences
- A defensible generic staging, supported by several insurers: (a) pre-contract licensing; (b) a 90-day orientation/fast-start block with mandatory courses and deadlines; (c) months 4-12 under a validated financing/bonus scheme with monthly or quarterly checkpoints; (d) year 2 as a retention risk period needing continued coaching; (e) a separate leader track of up to two years.
- Completion deadlines attached to e-learning (9 courses in 6 months; ROP1 in 90 days) show insurers treat course completion itself as a tracked milestone, alongside production validation.

### Gaps
- No programme names, phases or validation figures found for Muang Thai Life, Thai Life, Krungthai-AXA, Allianz Ayudhya, Prudential Thailand or Manulife (any market). Manulife surfaced only for leader-development programmes without detail.
- Actual validation numbers (cases, premium, persistency) and allowance amounts for any Thai programme: not found; these are usually in internal agency compensation circulars.
- What is taught in each phase of AIA FA 4.0 or FWD FutureMe: not found.
- The AIA "9 courses in 6 months" rule comes from a unit website, not AIA corporate; treat as indicative.

## 3. LIMRA/LOMA and industry guidance on onboarding sequence and field-leader training

### Takeaway
LIMRA frames onboarding as a first-year process with the heaviest emphasis on the first 90 days, and its research names early activity (fast start), pre-hire selection, joint field work, quality sales training and mentoring as the leading retention factors. Its leader curriculum (AMTC) has five modules: orientation and planning, recruiting, selection, training, and performance management.

### Cited Findings

**New-agent sequence**
- [V-extract] LIMRA "Fast Track to Sales": four phases beginning with a new-agent onboarding course; aims to position agents for success in the first 90 days; includes four mandatory micro digital-learning courses and a series of micro-assessments. An older page states the target as fundamentals for the first 90-180 days. Year not shown. — [LIMRA Fast Track to Sales flyer](https://www.limra.com/contentassets/39d2de35db1540e7abc9478118ca23b4/fast-track-to-sales-flyer.pdf); [LIMRA Fast Track to Sales (old page)](https://www.limra.com/en/old/fast-track-to-sales/)
- [V-extract] A 2026 LIMRA Distribution and Marketing Conference session states onboarding covers the first year, with focus on the first 90 days. — [LIMRA 2026 conference deck, session 4](https://www.limra.com/globalassets/limra-loma/events-learning-and-networking/conferences/2026/2026-distribution-and-marketing-conference/presentations/4.-empowering-new-financial-professionals_building-a-high-impact-onboarding-and-training-experience.pdf)
- [V-extract] LIMRA/Finseca research (2022): field and home-office leaders both chose five of twelve factors as having the greatest positive effect on two-year retention: early activity (fast start), a strong pre-hire selection process, joint field work, quality sales training, and mentoring. — [InsuranceNewsNet, 5 key factors](https://insurancenewsnet.com/innarticle/5-key-factors-that-help-retain-new-financial-professionals); [LIMRA 2022 release](https://www.limra.com/en/newsroom/news-releases/2022/five-ways-to-keep-financial-professionals-engaged/)
- [V-extract] Four-year retention: about 15% of financial professionals in agency systems recruiting mainly inexperienced people remained with the hiring company after four years (LIMRA figure for 2020; US-centred). ThinkAdvisor (2025) repeats roughly 15%. — [InsuranceNewsNet, A Path to Better Agent Retention](https://insurancenewsnet.com/innarticle/a-path-to-better-agent-retention); [ThinkAdvisor 2025](https://www.thinkadvisor.com/amp/2025/07/14/how-to-fix-the-agent-retention-crisis-in-life-insurance/)
- [V-extract] LIMRA 2025 conference deck: year 1 shows little retention difference because agents are supported by onboarding, coaching and training; the gap appears in year 2 (a "cliff") when first-year support falls away. — [LIMRA 2025 Annual Conference, session 16](https://www.limra.com/globalassets/limra-loma/events-learning-and-networking/conferences/2025/limra-annual-conference/session-16-building-high-performing-sales-teams.pdf)
- [V-extract] LOMA Asia analysis (2025): agents who achieve early wins within 90 days are far more likely to stay; joint fieldwork, mentoring and ongoing coaching help new agents bridge theory and practice. — [LOMA MarketFacts 2025, Retaining Life Agents in Asia](https://www.loma.org/en/news/marketfacts/2025/retaining-life-agents-in-asia-lessons-for-sustainable-growth)
- [V-extract] LIMRA and Hoopis Performance Network launched "Trustworthy Selling Quick Start" for new producers (2014). — [LIMRA 2014 release](https://www.limra.com/en/newsroom/news-releases/2014/limra-and-the-hoopis-performance-network-launch-trustworthy-selling-quick-start/)

**Field-leader training**
- [V-extract] AMTC (Agency Management Training Course) covers five modules: (1) orientation and planning, (2) recruiting top sales talent, (3) selecting new agents including presenting the career opportunity, (4) training for results, (5) performance management for sales growth. It develops 28 specific management skills. — [LIMRA AMTC page](https://limra.com/en/solutions-and-services/global/learning-and-development/programs-for-managers/agency-management-training-course-amtc/); [AMTC flyer](https://www.limra.com/siteassets/solutions-and-services/global-solutions/learning--development/amtc-flyer.pdf)
- [V-extract] AMTC delivery: modular (one topic at a time or the full course); one flyer cites a 13-day full format; a Traditional-Chinese flyer cites 23 weekly three-hour classes. The two formats conflict; confirm with LIMRA. — [AMTC flyer](https://www.limra.com/siteassets/solutions-and-services/global-solutions/learning--development/amtc-flyer.pdf); [AMTC flyer TC](https://www.limra.com/contentassets/24887906216a4980bb7252762162745f/amtc-flyer-tc.pdf)
- [V-extract] AMTC completion plus a capstone Summary Planning Project are requirements for the CIAM designation; AMTC is available in English, Greek, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Vietnamese (Thai not listed). — [LIMRA AIAM/CIAM flyer](https://www.limra.com/siteassets/solutions-and-services/global-solutions/learning--development/aiam-and-ciam-flyer.pdf); [LIMRA AMTC page](https://limra.com/en/solutions-and-services/global/learning-and-development/programs-for-managers/agency-management-training-course-amtc/)
- [V-extract] LIMRA "Fast Track to Management" (pre-managers): identifying training needs, delivering training sessions, giving new agents fundamental sales skills, coaching new agents on prospecting, and building a 90-day activity calendar for the manager's transition. — [LIMRA FT2M flyer](https://www.limra.com/siteassets/solutions-and-services/global-solutions/learning--development/programs-for-managers/fast-track-to-management/ft2m-flyer.pdf)
- [V-extract] FWD ran an intensive training programme for agency leaders in Asia. — [Insurance Business Asia](https://insurancebusinessmag.com/asia/news/breaking-news/fwd-launches-intensive-training-programme-for-agency-leaders-168598.aspx)

### Inferences
- LIMRA's evidence supports front-loading activity and joint field work in days 1-90 and deliberately extending coaching into months 13-24, since attrition concentrates after first-year support ends.
- A leader track can mirror AMTC's order (plan, recruit, select, train, manage performance), with a pre-manager stage first (FT2M content).
- The 15% four-year figure is US data; it should not be presented as a Thai benchmark.

### Gaps
- No LIMRA document was found that prescribes an explicit "30-60-90 day" checklist or the phrase "skills before product"; these are common industry practice but uncited here. Do not attribute them to LIMRA.
- Names of the four phases of Fast Track to Sales and the list of the 28 AMTC skills: not visible.
- Thailand-specific LIMRA/LOMA retention statistics: none found. The LOMA Asia article's figures could not be read.
- Whether AMTC is licensed or delivered in Thai by any Thai insurer: not found.

## 4. Skills / competencies typically certified at each stage

### Takeaway
No single source defines a stage-by-stage competency certification scheme; sources only show which skills appear in early programmes (needs analysis, presentation, closing, prospecting) and which topics regulators require (law/practice, product knowledge for unit-linked/UL). A stage-to-skill map must therefore be the designer's own construction, labelled as such.

- [V-extract] Prudential HK Fast Start content: orientation, selling skills, product knowledge, digital systems. — [Prudential HK training support](https://www.prudential.com.hk/jsp/prurecruit/en/Ideal-Platform/Training-Support/index.jsp)
